Layered wind-anisotropic noise

Isotropic fBm is “blobby.” Real dunes are stretched, sheared, and stacked by scale under a prevailing wind.

SNOWFLOW · desert heightfield

How it works

Compose three layers with different frequencies and amplitudes:

  • Macro — broad ridges (tens of m)
  • Meso — drifts sheared along wind (metres)
  • Micro — sastrugi / ripples (dm), strongly anisotropic

For anisotropy, sample noise in a frame aligned to wind: p' = (dot(p, wind), dot(p, perp) * stretch) so features elongate across the wind and compress along it (or vice versa).

H = a₀ n(p/λ₀) + a₁ n(shear(p)/λ₁) + a₂ n(shear(p)/λ₂)

Prompt match

“Do not use a single fBm octave stack… Encode a wind direction and let the medium and fine layers stretch and shear along it.”
