Analytic atmosphere / sky

Scrub the sun elevation and watch horizon warmth, zenith blue, and ground lighting stay coherent — why demos prefer models over static HDRIs.

SNOWFLOW · Long Silence scattering

How it works

Simplified single-scattering: denser atmosphere near the horizon, Rayleigh bias toward blue, sun disc + Mie-ish glow, ground albedo bounce. Not a full Nishita integrator, but the parameter coupling is the lesson.

  • Low sun → long warm light, blue fill in shadows
  • High sun → cooler daylight, shorter shadows
  • Aerial perspective: distant terrain loses contrast toward sky color

Prompt match

“Ambient light must be strongly blue-shifted. The cool-shadow and warm-light contrast is essential.”

Controls

0.45
Drop elevation near 0–15° — snow/desert demos live in that range for long readable shadows.
