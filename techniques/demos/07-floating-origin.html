Floating origin + precision

At large coordinates, float32 eats your sub-meter detail. Keep the player at 0 and move the world instead.

Long Silence precision · depth

How it works

  • Naive: ship world position → 1e6+; nearby props jitter
  • Floating origin: ship stays at (0,0,0); subtract ship position from every object each frame (or in chunks)
  • Log depth: remap depth so near and far nested spheres share resolution — custom shaders must opt in
p_render = p_world − player_world

What this demo shows

Two panels of a grid of thin poles. Left: positions placed at a huge origin offset (simulating far-from-origin). Right: same relative layout recentered. Crank the offset — left turns to spaghetti, right stays clean.
