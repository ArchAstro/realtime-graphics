Typical order

beauty (HDR)
→ bloom (threshold in exposed units)
→ tonemap (ACES / AgX)
→ grain + vignette
→ sharpen / FXAA

TAA (not in this simple demo) stabilizes glints and thin geo. Measure 1% lows, not only avg FPS.
